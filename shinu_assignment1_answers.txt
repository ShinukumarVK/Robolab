Topic latching

Observation — latch=False
The publisher published the message "System Ready" once on the topic /robot_status. 
When the subscriber was started after the publisher had already sent the message, the subscriber did not receive it.
Reason: With latch=False, 
ROS does not retain the previously published message for new subscribers. 
Therefore, the subscriber receives no message because it connected after the message publication.

Observation — latch=True

After changing the publisher's `latch` parameter to `True`, the publisher sent `"System Ready"` once on `/robot_status`. 
The subscriber was started several seconds later and successfully received the message.

**Reason:** When `latch=True`, ROS retains the last published message and sends it to newly connected subscribers. 
Therefore, the subscriber receives `"System Ready"` even though the message was published before it started.

Latching allows a late-joining subscriber to receive the publisher's most recently published message, provided the publisher remains active.
